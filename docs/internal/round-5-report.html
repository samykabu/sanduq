Outcome

Twenty of the 23 items shipped. The three that did not are caches. After the Codex plan review we agreed that a cache may only skip work on a path that does not decide evidence, containment or claim exclusion. Measured against that rule, none of the three saved time, so they were dropped or reverted. X14 stays deferred, as you decided.

The round makes Sanduq safer and easier to maintain; it does not make it faster. Call and git counters are equal before and after, and alternating full-tier runs are flat. The gains are correctness: eleven Codex findings and three live-run defects fixed, one shared GitHub transport that never retries a write, and a lint that catches a missed version bump.
