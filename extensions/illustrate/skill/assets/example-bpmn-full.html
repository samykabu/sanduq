Expense approval

One pool, three lanes, one decision. The manager's exclusive gateway sends each claim down exactly one of two routes: back to the employee, or on to Finance.

THE HEADLINE

Every claim ends one of two ways

The gateway is exclusive, so a claim takes the Rejected flow or the Approved flow, never both. Each route has its own end event.

Reading the notation

  • Thin circle starts, thick circle ends
  • Rounded box is a task; the figure marks a User Task
  • Diamond with an X is an exclusive gateway
  • Solid arrow is a sequence flow

Lanes say who acts

Each task and event sits in the lane of the role that owns it. Sequence flow may cross lanes but never leaves the pool.
